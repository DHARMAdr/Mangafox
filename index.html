      Settings

      CORS proxies

      Browsers block direct reading of other sites, so a proxy fetches the page for you. All proxies are asked at the same time; the first good answer wins. Free ones are slow or blocked at times. Your own proxy (see MANGAFLOW.md) is the reliable choice, and it is the only kind that can load hidden chapter lists.

        Reading & display

        Original and Balanced copy JPEG pages untouched. WebP pages must be converted (a PDF can not hold WebP). The download window shows the size of each choice first.

        While you read, MangaFlow loads the pictures of the next chapters by itself and tries again by itself when a picture or chapter did not load (internet hiccup). Uses more data and memory.
